Features

Built for big playlists.

A thousand-track playlist is a different job from one song. The app is designed for the long run: parallel, resumable, and honest about what failed.

Any format you want

Music at up to 320 kbps or lossless, or the full video up to 4K. Pick once in Settings; every download follows it.

MP3M4AOpusFLACWAVMP4MKVWebM

Tagged & named

Artist, title and album written into every file, cover art embedded, files named “Artist - Title”.

Picks up where it left off

Several downloads at once, automatic retries with backoff, and a saved queue. Close the app mid-run and carry on later.

Fill the gaps

Compare a folder you already own against a playlist and queue only what's missing.

Keeps itself current

Lives in your menu bar or system tray, and installs new versions in place when you say so.

How it works

Three steps. No command line.

  1. 01

    Install

    Download the zip for your computer, unzip and open it. Everything it needs is inside; the first run offers to fetch FFmpeg for you.

  2. 02

    Paste a link

    A playlist, album, single video or a text file of song names. Press / to jump to the paste box, or drag a link onto it.

  3. 03

    Download now

    Choose Music or Video and press the button. Watch each track land, and retry anything that fails with one click.

Privacy

What the app knows about you.

You sign in once, with Google or an email address. That ties downloads to a person, so when something breaks the developer can see it and help. Here's exactly what that means.

Recorded: your email address, the playlist links you download, and any errors.
Stays on your computer: the music and video files, your folders and your settings.
Downloads run locally. Files come straight to your machine, never through a server of ours.
Nothing else is collected. No ads, no tracking, no selling data.
