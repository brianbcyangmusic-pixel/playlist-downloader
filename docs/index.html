Everything you need, nothing you don't

A focused desktop app — fast, local, and respectful of your data.

🎵

Audio & video

MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC or keep the original. Up to 4K video, with quality presets.

🏷️

Tagged metadata

Artist, title, album, cover art embedded automatically. Files renamed to "Artist - Title".

⚡

Fast & parallel

Concurrent downloads and fragment fetching. Resumes interrupted runs via an archive.

🔍

Compare & fill gaps

Diff a local folder against a playlist and download only the missing tracks.

🔒

Runs locally

No account needed. Everything stays on your machine; nothing is uploaded.

🔄

Auto-updates

The menu bar / tray app checks GitHub Releases and updates itself in place.

Three steps to your music

No configuration, no Python, no command line.

Install

Download the zip for your system, unzip, and run. The backend is bundled.

Paste a link

Drop a YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify playlist URL into the app.

Choose & download

Pick audio or video, quality and format. Hit download and watch progress.

Requirements

Lightweight and self-contained.

🍎

macOS

Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 11+. Intel Macs: build from source.

🪟

Windows

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. No .NET or Python install required.

🎧

FFmpeg

Optional but recommended for converting to MP3/M4A. The app can fetch it for you.

Ready when you are

Free, open source, and updated regularly.
